生成方式How it was made

单文件 Three.js 场景，由 LLM 按一条提示词生成：盐壳、浅水、镜面天空与 3×5 时段/天气系统均程序化生成，无后端、无追踪、除 Three.js CDN 外无外部资源。

A single-file Three.js scene generated by an LLM from one prompt: salt crust, shallow water, mirrored sky and a 3 × 5 time/weather system, all procedural. No backend, no tracking, no external assets beyond the Three.js CDN.
